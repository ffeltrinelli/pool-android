# Computer-only cleanup — historical review, 24 October 2026

Solo mode (playing without a computer opponent) was removed to keep one match flow. Legacy play-mode preferences are ignored. Current rules and controls are in the [README](../README.md); subsequent eight-ball changes supersede the original scoring and breaker behavior.

The cleanup preserved gameplay tuning. Its key constraint was retaining selected computer shots across suspension without applying execution error—the difficulty-dependent variation in direction and power—twice. Validation and limitations remain in the [device record](device-validation.md#computer-only-play-and-clean-code-cleanup-2026-10-24).
