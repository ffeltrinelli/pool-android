# Eight-ball design

The game uses a simplified [WPA variant](../README.md#rules-of-this-game): no called pockets and automatic resolution of break choices. These choices keep casual touch play moving without referee dialogs.

Live play and silent opponent evaluation share rule decisions so planning cannot use a different interpretation of a legal shot. Physics reports facts; it does not assign groups or decide winners. Placement is confirmed separately from striking to avoid accidental shots while moving the cue ball.

The [24 October validation](device-validation.md#simplified-eight-ball-2026-10-24) records initial evidence and later fixes. Historical ball-count and fixed-break descriptions apply only to their recorded builds.
